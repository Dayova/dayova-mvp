## Inspection and limits

All four contact sheets for each final video and complete-timeline sampling of
both source recordings were inspected using `inspect-video-evidence`. The raw
dark recording covered 62.29 seconds with 62 samples at 1 fps / four sheets;
the raw light recording covered 145.02 seconds with 80 samples at approximately
0.552 fps / five sheets. Long idle tails were excluded from the published
continuous excerpts. No speed-up or stitched stages. Exports use 30 fps
encoding; native source cadence is variable. Sampling establishes the visible
sequence, not frame-by-frame smoothness or sub-0.5-second timing. Neither
video has audio; transcription is not applicable.

Telemetry was disabled locally because the development client otherwise fails
on the independent `Invalid analytics property: eas_channel` error. The initial
missing-key toast was dismissed and the development-menu floating button was
hidden before recording. No repository code was changed for those settings.
Published screens have no debugging overlays. These captures do not validate
analytics, iOS, enlarged text, or screen-reader behavior. Earlier iOS media is
historical and predates the header removal.

Media is attached to GitHub rather than committed to the repository. Private
test-account details and diagnostic logs are excluded from published evidence.
